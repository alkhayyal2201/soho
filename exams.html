How exams work

For staff: click an exam → enter your name → answer → Submit → instant score, per-question feedback, and pass/fail. Your best score is remembered on this device.

For admins: click Manage (top right) → create or edit exams with the visual form — or paste the clear JSON format. Saving publishes to the whole team instantly. No code changes.

Grading: Multiple-choice & True/False are exact-graded. Short answers use keyword matching (provisional score) and always show the model answer for review.
